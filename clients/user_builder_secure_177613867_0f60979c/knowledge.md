# Offer Overview

Acme Health AI helps outpatient clinics automate patient scheduling, intake reminders, and repetitive front-desk calls.

# Approved Value Points

- Reduces manual scheduling back-and-forth for common appointment types.
- Helps staff spend less time on repetitive inbound and outbound call handling.
- Supports multi-location workflows for growing practice groups.
- Can route common questions before they reach front-desk teams.

# Demo Talking Points

- Show how quickly a patient can be guided to the right appointment path.
- Highlight staff time savings and a smoother patient intake experience.
- Position the platform as an operations workflow tool, not a clinical decision tool.

# Proof Points

- Sample customer pilots reported fewer missed callbacks and faster scheduling response times.
- Teams often start with one location before rolling out more broadly.

# Pricing and Policy

- Pricing is customized after a discovery call.
- Detailed compliance, integration, and implementation questions should be routed to a specialist.
